# Thank you

OciDeck exists because people gave it their time, their ideas, and their
encouragement. Open source is never built by code alone. A sharp question, a
patient bug report, a translation, a nudge in a better direction, a word of
advice, or simply the conviction that something is worth doing — each of these
moves a project forward, and none of them show up in a commit graph.

So this page is here to say what a changelog cannot: **thank you.** To everyone
who has helped shape OciDeck, in ways large and small, seen and unseen — your
generosity is part of what the software is. Contributions and help are the
lifeblood of open source, and we do not take a single one of them for granted.

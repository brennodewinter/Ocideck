---

<!-- skip -->

# This is how you work with this template

- Choose one defined process problem and one measurable main result (**Y-01**).
- Use the questions on each guide slide as a checklist; then add regular slides for your answers.
- Replace the explanation in charter and CTQ tree with your project information, complete the SIPOC and indicate sources, owners and dates.
- Help slides are not presented or exported. If you want to show one, turn off **Skip** for that slide.

# Define

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Defining?

- What specific problem does the customer or user see, and since when?
- Who is experiencing the problem and what is the demonstrable effect?
- Where does the process begin and end; what is expressly outside the scope?
- What measurable outcome needs to improve (**Y-01**) and what is the desired level?
- Who decides, who implements and when will this phase be completed?

## Timeline

Record milestones, decision points and end dates.

When is the result demonstrably good and sustainable enough?

# Measure

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when measuring?

- How exactly is Y-01 calculated, in what units and with what limits?
- What is the current value and over what representative period was it measured?
- From what sources does the data come, who collects it and how often?
- Are definitions, measuring instruments and sample reliable enough?
- What breakdowns are needed to make differences visible?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Analyze

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Analyzing?

- Which patterns, outliers and differences are actually in the data?
- What possible causes do employees, customers and process data mention?
- Which root causes have been confirmed with evidence and which have not yet?
- How does each confirmed cause explain the effect on Y-01?
- Which cause deserves attention first and why?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Improve

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when improving?

- What solutions address a confirmed root cause?
- On what criteria do you compare effect, risk, costs and feasibility?
- What do you test first on a small scale, with what expectations and measurement period?
- What changed in Y-01 and what side effects occurred?
- Which solution are you implementing, who owns it and what is the implementation plan?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Control

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist - What do you record when Controlling?

- Who monitors Y-01 after transfer, how often and with what data source?
- Which boundary or trend requires intervention and who will act?
- Which method, instruction or system setting guarantees the change?
- How do you check that the effect lasts and when do you re-evaluate?
- What open risks, lessons and follow-up actions are you transferring?

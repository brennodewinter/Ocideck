# Decision-making meeting
## Meeting · Date · Chair · Attendees

---

# How this meeting works

- Every agenda item has one purpose: to inform, to discuss or to decide
- First the facts, then the views, only then the decision
- Decisions and actions are recorded on the spot, with an owner
- Objections are raised in the meeting, not afterwards

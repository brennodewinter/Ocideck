# GRIP escalation
## Incident · Date and time · Leader · Current GRIP level

---

# What GRIP escalation arranges

- One agreed structure to coordinate an incident
- The level sets who is in charge and who joins
- Scaling up and down are both deliberate decisions
- The higher the level, the wider the effect and governance

---

<!-- _class: table -->

# GRIP levels

| Level | Scope | Coordination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GRIP 1 | Source control | On-scene command post |
| GRIP 2 | Source and effect area | Operational team |
| GRIP 3 | Governance, municipality | Municipal policy team |
| GRIP 4 | Multiple municipalities | Regional policy team |

---

# Current situation and picture

- What is going on and since when?
- Which area and which parties are affected?
- Why does the current GRIP level fit?
- What could require scaling up or down?

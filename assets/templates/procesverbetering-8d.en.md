# Process improvement: 8D project

---

<!-- skip -->

# This is how you work with this template

- Use 8D for a serious or recurring problem and choose one measurable key outcome (**Y-01**).
- Use the questions on each guide slide as a checklist; then add regular slides for your answers.
- Replace the explanation in the charter and CTQ tree with your project information, complete the SIPOC and separate temporary containment from permanent correction.
- Help slides are not presented or exported. If you want to show one, turn off **Skip** for that slide.

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D0: Prepare

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D0?

- What is the first factual signal and how serious or urgent is it?
- Which customer, safety, delivery or business operations can be affected?
- Is 8D appropriate given complexity, repetition and required disciplines?
- Which data, samples and traces need to be secured immediately?
- What immediate action is needed before the team is complete?

---

<!-- _class: canvas -->
<!-- ocideck_template: charter -->

# Projectcharter

## Problem

Summarize the first signal without citing an unproven cause.

## Goal

Record the desired Y-01 outcome and the required recovery date.

## Scope

Define product, process, location, period and excluded cases.

## Team

Name owner and required areas of expertise; work out the names at D1.

## Timeline

Establish deadlines for containment, root cause analysis, correction and closure.

## Success criteria
When is the customer protected, the cause removed and recurrence prevented?

# Measurable customer requirements (CTQ tree)

- What outcome needs to be restored for the customer? — **Y-01**
  - Which requirement or limit is not being met?
  - What additional condition should be retained?

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — How do you complete the SIPOC?

- Define the process where the problem arises or escapes.
- Start with affected customer and abnormal output.
- Summarize the process in 4 to 7 main steps; don't add causes yet.
- Record inputs and suppliers to reveal possible source and transfer points.
- Later use evidence from D4 to confirm causes.

# D1: Build the team

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D1?

- Who has process knowledge, product knowledge, data, decision-making authority and customer contact?
- Who owns the 8D result and who leads the day-to-day approach?
- What roles and responsibilities does each team member have?
- What capacity and authority does the team need?
- How often does the team meet and where are decisions recorded?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D2: Describe the problem

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D2?

- What exactly is wrong, where, when, how often and to what extent?
- Who or which products does it occur with — and where does it not occur?
- What is the current Y-01 value compared to requirement or normal performance?
- What photos, measurements, examples and source data support the description?
- Which cause assumptions do you consciously leave out of the problem sentence?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D3: Implement containment actions

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D3?

- Which temporary action immediately protects the customer or process against the effect?
- Where does the promotion apply, from when and who will implement it?
- How has it been checked that the containment actually works?
- What risks, costs and limitations does the temporary measure entail?
- When and under what conditions will the restriction end?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D4: Identify and verify root causes

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D4?

- What cause explains why the problem arose?
- What escape cause explains why it was not discovered sooner?
- What evidence makes each cause reproducible or testable?
- What suspicions have been investigated and rejected?
- How do the confirmed causes affect Y-01?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D5: Choose and verify corrective actions

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D5?

- Which final action removes which confirmed root or escape cause?
- Which alternatives have been compared for effect, risk and feasibility?
- How has the chosen action been tested before widespread implementation?
- What was the measured effect on Y-01 and which side effects occurred?
- Who approves the chosen correction?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D6: Implement and validate corrective actions

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D6?

- Which actions were definitively introduced where, when and by whom?
- Which procedures, systems, training and controls have been adapted?
- How and when will the temporary restriction be safely phased out?
- Which results prove that Y-01 sustainably meets the requirement?
- What deviations or open points still need to be resolved?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D7: Prevent recurrence

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D7?

- Where else could the same cause or escape route occur?
- What standards, design rules, controls, or lessons need to be adapted more broadly?
- Who carries out these preventive actions and when?
- How do you check that the change also works outside this case?
- What knowledge is shared with other teams or suppliers?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# D8: Recognize the team

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at D8?

- What evidence shows that problem, cause, correction, and prevention have been completed?
- Has the customer or process owner accepted the result?
- Which contribution and collaboration deserve explicit recognition?
- What lessons will the team take with them to the next problem?
- Who will formally close the file and where can the evidence be found?

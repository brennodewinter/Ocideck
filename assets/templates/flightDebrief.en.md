# Purpose and ground rules

- The pilot self-evaluates first, then the instructor adds to it
- Facts on the table first, judgement only after that
- Just culture: errors are learning material, not blame
- Also works as a solo review without an instructor
- Memory aid: your school's and instructor's requirements always come first

# TEM as a lens

- Threat: what came at the flight from outside?
- Error: where did we slip up ourselves?
- Undesired state: when did the picture stop being right?
- Focus on the recovery, not just the moment itself

# (A)MIST trauma handover
## Prehospital → ED · Dispatch · Date and time

---

# The letters of (A)MIST

- **A — Age:** age (and sex) of the patient
- **T — Time:** time of the incident and expected arrival
- **M — Mechanism:** the mechanism of injury
- **I — Injuries:** confirmed and suspected injuries
- **S — Signs:** vital signs and findings
- **T — Treatment:** treatment given and its effect

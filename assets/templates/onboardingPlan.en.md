# Onboarding plan (30-60-90 days)
## New hire · Role · Guide · Start date

---

# Purpose of onboarding

- Quickly find your way around people, systems and ways of working
- Clear, achievable goals per phase
- Someone who helps you and whom you can turn to
- From shadowing to contributing independently

---

<!-- _class: timeline -->

# From day 1 to day 90

- Day 1 :: Welcome :: People, workplace and access.
- Week 1 :: Orientation :: Team, systems and first tasks.
- 30 days :: Shadowing :: Learn the work and the context.
- 60 days :: Independent :: Pick up and finish tasks yourself.
- 90 days :: Contributing :: Fully up to speed in the role.

# Prepare a conversation
## Who · Topic · Date

---

# What I want to achieve

- My goal: what do I want this conversation to deliver?
- What is enough: which outcome am I content with?
- My fallback: what do I do if we can't work it out?

---

# The other person

- What I already know: about the person and about the topic
- Their interest: what do they want to get out of this conversation?
- Where they stand: rushed, worried, well-disposed?
- What I don't know and would rather ask than assume

---

<!-- _class: table -->

# The structure

| Phase | What I do there |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning | Make contact and say why I want this conversation |
| Core | Bring my key points and ask my questions |
| Closing | Summarize, make agreements, and name the follow-up |

# Agreements and follow-up

- Summarize at the end what we have agreed
- Record: who does what, and by when
- Agree how and when we will revisit this
- Check whether the other person understands the agreements the way I do

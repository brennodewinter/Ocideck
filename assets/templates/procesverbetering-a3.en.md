# Process improvement: A3 project

---

<!-- skip -->

# This is how you work with this template

- Tell one improvement story and keep the problem, goal and main result (**Y-01**) the same everywhere.
- Use the questions on each guide slide as a checklist; then add regular slides for your answers.
- Replace the explanation in the charter and CTQ tree with your project information, complete the SIPOC and separate facts from assumptions.
- Help slides are not presented or exported. If you want to show one, turn off **Skip** for that slide.

# Background

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record in Background?

- Why does this problem deserve attention now?
- Which customer, user or organizational objective is affected?
- Which facts show size, development and urgency?
- Which previous actions or decisions are relevant?
- What does the reader need to understand before you define the problem?

# Projectcharter

## Problem

Describe the difference between current and desired situation, with facts.

## Goal

Write down a measurable Y-01 target value and desired date.

## Scope

Define process boundaries, location or team involved, and exclusions.

## Team

Name owner, implementers, decision makers and involved users.

## Timeline

Record analysis, trial, introduction and evaluation moment.

## Success criteria
When has the problem been demonstrably solved and secured?

# Checklist — How do you complete the SIPOC?

- Start with customer and output; This way the problem remains viewed from the needs perspective.
- Summarize the investigated process in 4 to 7 main steps.
- Note inputs and suppliers that make the process dependent.
- Use the same start and end boundaries as in the charter.
- Put details and decision points later in a flowchart, not in the SIPOC.

# Current condition

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record in the Current situation?

- How does the process work now, seen in the workplace and not just according to the instructions?
- What is the current Y-01 value and how does it vary over time or groups?
- Where do waiting times, errors, repair work or transmission loss occur?
- Which facts have been measured and which observations are still anecdotal?
- What is the concrete gap with the desired state?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Target condition

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record for the Target Condition?

- Which method and outcome should be visible on the target date?
- Which Y-01 target value and intermediate steps are included?
- Which preconditions must be maintained, such as safety or quality?
- What is deliberately not covered by this improvement?
- How and by whom is it determined that the target condition has been achieved?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Root-cause analysis

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist - What do you record during the Cause Analysis?

- What direct causes explain the gap between current and desired state?
- Why can these causes arise or persist?
- What evidence confirms each root cause?
- Which popular explanations have actually been refuted?
- Which controllable root causes do the countermeasures target?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Countermeasures

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record for Countermeasures?

- Which measure addresses which confirmed root cause?
- What alternatives have been considered and why is this choice better?
- What expected effect will the measure have on Y-01?
- Which risks or unwanted side effects do you need to manage?
- What is first carried out as a trial before the approach is widely introduced?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Implementation plan

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record in the Implementation Plan?

- Which concrete action is carried out by whom and when?
- What resources, consent, communication and training are needed?
- What dependencies can block scheduling?
- When do you measure Y-01 in the meantime and who assesses the outcome?
- What is the fallback option when a measure does not work?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Follow-up

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record in Follow-up?

- Which Y-01 results were measured after implementation compared to baseline measurement and target?
- Which measure worked, which did not and what did you learn from it?
- Which standard, instruction or responsibility has been adjusted?
- Who monitors the result and when is it checked again?
- What remaining gap or new question will receive a next improvement cycle?

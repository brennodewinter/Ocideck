# SBAR handover
## Patient · Ward · From whom to whom · Date and time

---

# The four steps of SBAR

- **S — Situation:** what is going on right now, short and concrete
- **B — Background:** the relevant background and history
- **A — Assessment:** your judgement of what is happening
- **R — Recommendation:** what you ask for or advise

---

# Situation (S)

- Who are you and which ward are you calling from?
- Which patient is this (name, age, room)?
- What is the acute problem or reason for the handover?
- Since when has this been going on?

---

# Background (B)

- Reason for admission and main diagnosis: …
- Relevant history and allergies: …
- Current medication and treatment: …
- What has been done so far?

---

# Assessment (A)

- Current vital signs (BP, pulse, respiration, temp, SpO₂): …
- Your read on the severity and the picture: …
- What worries you or is changing?
- Possible causes: …

---

# Recommendation (R)

- What do you concretely ask for or advise?
- Within what timeframe is action needed?
- Who does what, and when will you report back?
- Agreements on re-evaluation: …

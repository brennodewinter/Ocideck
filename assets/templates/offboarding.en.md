# Purpose of a proper offboarding

- Hand over knowledge and ongoing work well
- Revoke access and resources carefully
- Say goodbye on good terms
- Learn from what the leaver shares

# Exit interview

- Why are you leaving, and since when did that play?
- What went well and gave energy?
- What could improve in work, team or organisation?
- Would you come back or recommend us?

# IMSAFE fitness-to-fly check
## Pilot · Flight · Date · This check does not replace medical judgement

---

# The six factors of IMSAFE

- **I — Illness:** am I ill or not quite fit?
- **M — Medication:** am I on medication that affects me?
- **S — Stress:** is there tension that distracts me?
- **A — Alcohol:** enough time since the last drink?
- **F — Fatigue:** am I rested and alert?
- **E — Eating / Emotion:** eaten well and emotionally stable?

---

- [ ] Stress: no tension distracting me from the flight

- [ ] Fatigue: rested, alert and not overtired
- [ ] Eating / Emotion: eaten, hydrated and emotionally stable

# Doubt means No-Go

- If in doubt about any factor: do not fly
- Ask an instructor or aviation medical examiner
- Postpone or let someone else fly
- A missed flight beats an unsafe flight

---

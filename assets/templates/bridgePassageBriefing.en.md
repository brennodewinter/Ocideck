# The four steps of the passage plan (APEM)

- **Appraisal:** gather all information for the voyage
- **Planning:** set the route, waypoints and no-go areas
- **Execution:** carry out the voyage as planned
- **Monitoring:** watch position and deviation continuously
- Around it: Bridge Resource Management — working together on the bridge

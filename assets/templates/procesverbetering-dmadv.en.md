# Process improvement: DMADV project

---

<!-- skip -->

# This is how you work with this template

- Use DMADV for a new or fundamentally redesigned process and choose one measurable customer outcome (**Y-01**).
- Use the questions on each guide slide as a checklist; then add regular slides for your answers.
- Replace the explanation in the charter and CTQ tree with your project information, complete the SIPOC and make requirements testable before you design.
- Help slides are not presented or exported. If you want to show one, turn off **Skip** for that slide.

# Define

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Defining?

- Which customer or user has which unmet need?
- Why is a new design necessary and why is improving the existing process not enough?
- What outcome should the design deliver (**Y-01**) and within what scope?
- Who decides on requirements, design choices and release?
- What planning, preconditions and success criteria apply?

# Projectcharter

## Problem or opportunity

Describe the unmet need, target group and demonstrable reason.

## Goal

Formulate the desired outcome in a measurable and time-bound manner.

## Scope

Note the starting point, end point, points of contact and what falls outside the design.

## Team

Name client, design owner, users and required experts.

## Timeline

Record milestones, decision gates and intended deployment.

## Success criteria
When does the design demonstrably meet customer needs?

# Checklist — How do you complete the SIPOC?

- Start with the **Customer**: who uses the new process result?
- Then identify the required **Output** and 4 to 7 intended **Process** steps.
- Note the required **Input** and the **Supplier** that makes each input available.
- Keep a general overview; design details will follow later.
- Check whether the selected boundaries correspond to charter and Y-01.

# Measure

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when measuring?

- Which customer needs have been translated into measurable requirements and priorities?
- What are target value, lower or upper limit, unit and measurement method of Y-01?
- What use cases, volumes and exceptions should the design be able to handle?
- Which existing achievements or alternatives do you use as a reference?
- How will you objectively test whether each requirement has been met?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Analyze

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Analyzing?

- What functions must the process fulfill to meet the requirements?
- What relationships and trade-offs exist between customer wishes, risks and design features?
- What assumptions still need to be explored or tested?
- Which failure modes and dependencies are most important?
- What minimum design criteria must every solution meet?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Design

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record in Design?

- Which design variants were considered and on what criteria were they compared?
- What does the chosen process flow look like, including roles, systems and transfers?
- How does the design prevent or control the major failure modes?
- What does a prototype or test teach about operation and ease of use?
- Which variant goes to verification, with which open points?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Verify

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Verifying?

- Which test proves for each requirement that the design works under realistic conditions?
- What results have been achieved and what deviations remain?
- What do users and process owners think of operation and feasibility?
- What control, instruction and measurement are required after commissioning?
- Who releases the design and on the basis of what evidence?

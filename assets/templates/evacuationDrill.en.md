## Site · Date · Drill leader · Scenario

---

# Purpose of the drill

- Test whether the evacuation runs smoothly and on time
- Responders and staff know their task
- Routes, signals and assembly point work
- Gather improvements without blaming anyone

---

# Scenario

- What is the trigger (fire, gas leak, threat)?
- Where does it start and how does it develop?
- Which assumptions apply to this drill?
- What do we deliberately practise and not?

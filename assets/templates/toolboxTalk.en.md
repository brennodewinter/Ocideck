# Toolbox talk / last-minute risk analysis
## Work · Location · Date · Crew

---

# Why this toolbox / LMRA

- Just before the work starts, pause together on the risks
- Everyone knows the task, the hazards and the controls
- Anyone may and must stop the work if it is unsafe
- The LMRA is the final check on the spot itself

# Stop criteria

- The situation differs from what we discussed here
- A control is missing or does not work
- Weather, surroundings or third parties make it unsafe
- In doubt: stop and consult first, only then continue

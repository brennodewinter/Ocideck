# Internship presentation
## Company · Programme · Period · Name

---

# The company and the department

- What does the company do and for whom?
- Where does the department sit in the organisation?
- Who did I work with (roles, not names)?
- What was my place within the team?

---

# The assignment and the question behind it

- What was the assignment in one sentence?
- Which problem or need lies behind it?
- What was in and out of scope?
- What did the result have to meet?

---

<!-- _class: timeline -->

# Approach in phases

- Week 1 :: Explore :: Getting to know the company, the department and the assignment.
- Week 3 :: Investigate :: Digging into the question behind the assignment and gathering requirements.
- Week 6 :: Execute :: Building, testing and checking in with the supervisor along the way.
- Week 10 :: Finish :: Delivering, handing over and completing the report.

---

# The result

- What is delivered (product, advice, research)?
- How was it validated or tested?
- What is the company already using?
- How was it handed over and safeguarded?

# This is how you work with this template

- Choose a small, visible problem and one measurable main result (**Y-01**).
- Use the questions on each guide slide as a checklist; then add regular slides for your answers.
- Replace the explanation in the charter and CTQ tree with your project information, complete the SIPOC and work with one small experiment at a time.
- Help slides are not presented or exported. If you want to show one, turn off **Skip** for that slide.

# Plan

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record at Plan?

- What exactly goes wrong, for whom and how often?
- What is the current value of Y-01 and what target condition do you want to achieve?
- Where does this small area of ​​improvement begin and end?
- What do you think the cause is and what evidence supports that?
- What small experiment can quickly and safely test that assumption?

# Checklist — How do you complete the SIPOC?

- Name the recipient and desired output of the process part.
- Summarize the current working method in 4 to 7 main steps.
- Note required inputs and who provides them.
- Use the overview to find transfers, waiting time and unclear expectations.
- Focus only on the part that the team can influence in this cycle.

# Do

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record during Execution?

- What exactly changed, where, when and by whom?
- Was the test carried out as planned? if not, what was different?
- What measurements and observations were collected during the trial?
- What unexpected effects, risks or reactions occurred?
- What needs to be adjusted immediately before the trial goes ahead?

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Check

---

<!-- skip -->

# Checklist — What do you record when Checking?

- How does the new Y-01 value compare to baseline measurement and target condition?
- What part of the expectation came true and what part did not?
- What did team and users learn about cause and solution?
- Do you choose to safeguard, adjust, test again or stop — and why?
- Who processes the chosen method and when will the next check take place?

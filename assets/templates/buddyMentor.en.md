# Buddy / mentor plan
## Newcomer · Buddy or mentor · Start date

---

# Role of the buddy or mentor

- Be a fixed, easy-to-reach point of contact
- Explain the unwritten rules and habits
- Think along without taking over the work
- Notice when something is not going well

---

# The newcomer's goals

- What do you want to learn in the first months?
- Where do you want to become independent quickly?
- What still feels daunting or unclear?
- How do you like to receive guidance?

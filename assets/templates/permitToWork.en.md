# Permit to work
## Work · Location · Valid from–to · Permit issuer

---

# What a permit to work is for

- For high-risk work that needs authorisation and isolation
- Think of hot work, heights, confined spaces or energy
- The permit records what is allowed, under which conditions
- Without release and sign-off the work does not start

---

# The work

- What are we going to do and where exactly?
- Who carries it out and who supervises?
- From when to when is the permit valid?
- Which adjacent activities are going on?

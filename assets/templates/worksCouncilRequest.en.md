# Advice or consent

- Request for advice (Works Councils Act, art. 25) or for consent (art. 27)?
- The proposed decision in one sentence: …
- Why now: the advice must still be able to genuinely influence the decision
- Who is at the table: director, works council and any advisers

---

# The proposed decision

- What exactly is the proposed decision (scope and parts)?
- What changes in the organisation or way of working?
- What is still open and where does the council have room?
- Proposed, not taken: the advice carries weight

---

# Rationale

- Why this decision: cause and goal: …
- Which alternatives were considered and why were they dropped?
- What happens if we do nothing?
- Which documents support this? Appendices: …

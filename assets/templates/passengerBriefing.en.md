# Passenger briefing (general aviation)
## Aircraft · Flight · Pilot in command

---

# Why this briefing

- The pilot in command is responsible for safety on board
- A well-informed passenger stays calm when something unexpected happens
- Questions are always welcome — before, during and after the flight
- The flight manual (AFM/POH) and this aircraft's briefing card take precedence

---

# Around the aircraft

- Always stay clear of the propeller, even with the engine stopped
- Board and exit only when the pilot in command says so
- Follow the designated walking route to and from the aircraft
- Baggage is secured; loose items do not belong in the cabin

---

# Seat belts and seats

- Belt on when boarding; release only after permission

- Seat and backrest locked during take-off and landing
- Hands off the controls, feet away from the pedals

# Fire, smoke and forced landing

- Smoke or fire: report it, stay calm, follow instructions
- Brace position for a forced landing: lean forward, protect your head
- After stopping: belts off, exit on the side indicated

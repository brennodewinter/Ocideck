# Just culture: report to learn

- We report to learn, not to punish
- Facts come first, not the question of blame
- An honest report of an honest mistake is not held against you
- Only intent or gross negligence crosses the line

# What keeps this blame-free

- An honest mistake in a complex situation is human
- We look for the cause in the system, not the person
- The line is deliberate risk, intent or gross negligence
- The report stays within the agreed confidentiality

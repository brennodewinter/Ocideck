# Purpose and ground rules

- We learn from this operation; we are not looking for someone to blame
- Facts first, opinions later: describe what you saw and did
- Everyone gets to speak; rank plays no role
- What is shared here stays within the team
- Short and concrete: we close with agreed actions

---

# What was the plan
